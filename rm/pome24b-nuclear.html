Pome24B · Energy and the Environment

Nuclear Energy

How does a nuclear power plant produce electricity? A gap-fill text, a video and tasks to practise the technical vocabulary.

How to use this page

Work through the page from top to bottom: first the gap-fill text in your book, then the video, then the tasks A to F. You can watch the video here or scan the QR code to watch it on your phone. Pause it as often as you need.

Drag the words into the right places (on a phone: tap a word, then tap the place) and answer the questions in complete sentences. Your answers are saved in this browser. At the bottom, click “Check my answers” to see your result and the model answers.

1

Nuclear power: the gap-fill text

Read the text “Nuclear power” in your book (Unit 9, Energy and the environment, pages 130–131) and put in the missing words from the box. (If you have not done this yet, do it first before you continue.)

2

Watch the video

Watch the video “How does a nuclear power plant work?” (ENGIE Belgium). You can watch it here, or scan the QR code to watch it on your phone. Watch it more than once if you need to, and pause it while you work on the tasks.

Watch on your phone

Scan the QR code with your phone camera to open the video on YouTube.

youtube.com/watch?v=jpDRfaWYk3I

3

Tasks to the video

Work through tasks A to F. Drag the words into the right places (on a phone: tap a word, then tap the place). Answer the questions in complete sentences. Your answers are saved in this browser. When you are done, click Check my answers at the bottom.
