How to use this page

These exercises help you prepare for the exam on Study Packet II: Properties of Materials (OneNote: 2. Properties of Materials). Work through the parts in any order. Your answers are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back later.

  • Drag and drop: drag a word or definition into the right place. On a phone or tablet you can also tap a word first and then tap the place where it belongs. Drag it back to the box to remove it.
  • Writing tasks: answer in complete sentences, as in the exam.
  • When you are done, click Check my answers at the bottom. Drag-and-drop and true/false tasks are corrected. For writing tasks you see a model answer: compare it with your own text. There is more than one correct way to answer.
