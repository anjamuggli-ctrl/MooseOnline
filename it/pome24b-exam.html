Practice Exercises: Energy and the Environment

Practice exercises: Energy and the Environment

These practice exercises cover the entire Unit 9 and help you prepare for the exam. They are not the exam itself: the exam will look different, but it tests the same content and skills. So if you can do these exercises well, you are well prepared.

The exercises cover all the pages of Unit 9 that come in the exam: the greenhouse effect (p. 119), rising sea levels (pp. 122–123), converting energy and solar power (pp. 124–126), water power (pp. 126–128), wind power (p. 129), nuclear power and the island project (pp. 130–131). Section 7 trains you to read and interpret statistics.

  • Work section by section. At the end of each section, click Check answers to see your result for this section.
  • Drag the words into the right places (on a phone: tap a word, then tap the place). Answer the open questions in complete sentences, then compare with the model answers.
  • Questions marked Critical thinking or Prediction have no single correct answer. Good reasons are what count.
  • Your answers are saved in this browser. At the very end, you can check all sections at once.
