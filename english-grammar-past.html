Grammar notes: Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

Signal words · Present Perfect
everneveralreadyjustyetso farup to nowsince …for … (until now)recentlytodaythis week / year
Present Perfect
Past Simple
Started in the past – still going onThe situation isn’t over yet.

We have lived in Chur since 2019. (We still live there.)

Started and ended in the pastThe situation is completely over.

We lived in Bern for five years. (Then we moved.)

Life experience – up to nowWhen exactly isn’t important; it could happen again.

My aunt has climbed the Matterhorn twice.

A closed chapter of lifeThe person has died or that time of life is over.

My grandfather climbed the Matterhorn twice.

Past action – result you see nowThe effect still matters at this moment.

I ’ve forgotten my laptop! (I can’t work now.)

Past action – no result nowIt’s simply a story about the past.

I forgot my laptop last week, but a colleague lent me hers.

Time period not finished yettoday, this morning, this week, this year …

I have had three coffees today.

Time period finishedyesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2023 …

I had three coffees yesterday.

Signal words · Past Simple
yesterdaylast week / year… agoin 2023when …?thenafter thatat 8 o’clockon Mondaythe other dayas a child
Form · Present Perfect

have / has + past participle

she has worked · they have gone
Form · Past Simple

verb + -ed / 2nd form (irregular)

she worked · they went
Watch out!

✗ I have seen him yesterday.   ✓ I saw him yesterday.

Finished time word → always Past Simple! German «Ich habe ihn gestern gesehen» is Past Simple in English.

Practice: Present Perfect vs. Past Simple

Present perfect or past simple?

Write the verb in brackets in the present perfect or the past simple. Look for the signal words!

  1. I (install) the new software yesterday.
  2. We (never / use) this program before.
  3. She (forget) her password twice this week.
  4. They (set up) the computer lab last year.
  5. He (repair) the printer yesterday afternoon.
  6. I (already / send) you the updated file.
  7. My colleague (write) that code two days ago.
  8. The students (not / finish) their project yet.
  9. She (restart) her laptop three times today.
  10. We (not / test) the new app last week.
  11. The IT team (fix) the network problem last night.
  12. I (just / upload) the photos to the cloud.
  13. He (attend) a cybersecurity workshop in 2022.
  14. We (already / learn) about databases in this course.
  15. She (delete) the wrong folder yesterday.
  16. The company (launch) a new website recently.
  17. I (connect) to Wi-Fi a few minutes ago.
  18. They (never / design) a website before this class.
  19. We (update) the operating system last Friday.
  20. He (just / recover) the lost data.
