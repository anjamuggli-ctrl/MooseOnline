Grammar notes: Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

Signal words · Present Simple
alwaysusuallynormallyoftensometimesrarelyneverevery day / weekonce a weekon Mondaysat 9:08 (timetable)
Present Simple
Present Continuous
Habits & routinesThings that happen again and again.

I take the bus to work every morning.

Happening right nowThe action is in progress at this moment.

Look! It ’s snowing in Chur!

Facts & permanent situationsAlways or generally true.

Water boils at 100 °C. Sara lives in Chur.

Temporary situationsOnly for a limited time – not forever.

This winter, I ’m working in a ski hotel.

Timetables & schedules (future)Fixed by an official plan.

The train to Zurich leaves at 9:08.

Personal plans (future)Arrangements you have already made.

We ’re going skiing on Saturday.

Stative verbs (Zustandsverben)Feelings, thoughts, senses, possession – no -ing!

I know the answer. This pizza tastes great.

Changes & trendsSomething is developing around now.

Prices are going up these days.

Signal words · Present Continuous

Form · Present Simple

I / you / we / they work · he / she / it works

Don’t forget the -s with he / she / it!
Form · Present Continuous

am / is / are + verb-ing

she is working · they are going
Watch out!

✗ I am knowing the answer.   ✓ I know the answer.

Stative verbs: know, like, love, hate, want, need, believe, understand, own, belong …

Practice: Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

Present simple or present continuous?

Write the verb in brackets in the present simple or the present continuous. Watch out for stative verbs! Some sentences have two gaps.

  1. I usually (wake up) at seven, but today I (sleep) in.
  2. She always (wear) glasses.
  3. Listen! The students (discuss) the new project.
  4. Our English class usually (end) at half past ten.
  5. The IT department (install) new software on all the computers right now.
  6. He (not / like) chocolate.
  7. They (believe) this program is very useful.
  8. Look! The technician (repair) the server.
  9. I (not / watch) TV at the moment, I (do) my homework.
  10. On Fridays we always (have) a team meeting.
  11. What (you / do) with my laptop right now?
  12. The printer usually (work) fine, but today it (not / print).
  13. He often (forget) his password.
  14. Shhh! She (record) the podcast at the moment.
  15. Our company (send) an update email every Monday.
  16. Look! Someone (hack) into the system!
  17. I (not / understand) this exercise.
  18. The children usually (play) outside after school.
  19. She (study) French this year at evening classes.
  20. Right now, my friend (download) a huge file.
